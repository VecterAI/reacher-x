## What changed

In this example release, the review screen keeps the original question beside the draft. A reviewer can compare the two without opening another panel.

- **More context:** the question stays visible while you read the reply.
- **Clearer status:** an unsaved review is marked before you leave.
- **Fewer interruptions:** a saved item stays in place until you move on.

## What stays the same

A person still decides whether the reply is useful. The screen does not send anything when a draft appears.

### A small wording change

Before: “Action completed.”

After: “Review saved.”

```text title="Example confirmation"
Review saved. Nothing has been sent.
```
