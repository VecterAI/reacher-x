ReacherX v3.0 is available in public beta. Describe what you sell or do, find people talking about a problem you can solve, and reply without leaving the app.

<BlogCallout title="From the October 2025 release">

This article adapts our original v3.0 launch thread. It describes that release, including its single-workspace limit. ReacherX has changed since then. The launch offer for the first 100 people is historical, not a current pricing offer.

## Start with what you sell

During onboarding, describe your product, service, or skill. That becomes your workspace description. ReacherX uses it to suggest keywords that can help you find customers.

You can use those suggestions or enter your own keywords. Start with language a customer would use when asking for help.

## Choose how closely to match

Turn on Exact Phrase Match when you want results containing a particular phrase. Turn it off to explore broader results.

## Read the context, then reply

Open a result to understand the person and their post. If you can help, write a relevant reply in the composer. You can keep writing while earlier replies send.

## Keep your workspace relevant

Update your workspace when you want to find customers for a different product, service, or skill. This release supports one workspace per account. Multiple workspaces were planned for a later update.

Choose posts, replies, or quotes. Use advanced filters to narrow the list, then change the sort order to find the conversations you want to read first.

## What we planned next

At launch, the next steps were multiple workspaces, more platforms, and less manual work. We wanted ReacherX to keep listening for customer needs and help with the conversations that followed.
