_Example post for reviewing the blog design. The scenario and publication date are illustrative._

A question at the top of a conversation is only the beginning. A reply further down may explain that the person already tried your suggestion, works under a different constraint, or has solved the problem.

Reading that extra reply can save both people an unnecessary exchange.

> Before adding a suggestion, check whether the conversation has already moved past it.

This short example intentionally has no section headings. It lets you inspect the article layout when there is no table of contents.
