You can search for an outreach tool and end up comparing an inbox, a contact database, a sales agent, and a marketing platform. They may all mention AI, but they solve different parts of the job.

Start with what is missing from your day. Do you need to find someone, understand whether they fit, write to them, or keep up with people you already know?

I build ReacherX. This guide covers 25 products people may consider alongside it. Official pages were checked September 10, 2026. The detailed articles explain documented workflows, not results from a benchmark or hands-on trials of every product.

There isn't enough public detail there to fairly compare channel availability, prices, and controls in a full article. Confirm those in a product demo before relying on them.

[Koala](https://getkoala.com/) is another relevant research option. Its current site describes bringing together first-party and third-party signals, account scoring, enrichment, and sales plays. Examine it when activity from your website, product, or existing data systems is central to deciding whom to contact. That is a different starting point from an empty research or hiring workspace.

[Reavion](https://www.reavion.com/) presents an autonomous browser for outbound and social activity. The public page invites people to a waiting list and shows broad network ambitions. Confirm which actions and networks are available to your account before treating those descriptions as a tested feature list. This guide doesn't turn an advertised ambition into a claim of proven coverage.

## Some tools belong to a different part of the work

These three can be useful without replacing the job ReacherX does:

- [GTM Arena](https://www.gtmarena.ai/) currently compares GTM data-vendor pricing. Use it when researching data costs; it isn't itself the outreach application those vendors feed.
- [Inflection](https://www.inflection.io/) focuses on B2B marketing automation, customer context, and lifecycle campaigns with agents. Investigate it for running marketing across a customer journey.
- [Juma](https://juma.ai/) provides AI marketing flows, shared project context, and collaboration. It is relevant when producing and coordinating marketing work is the bottleneck.

[Runable](https://runable.com/) is broader still: its site covers creating websites, campaigns, content, and tools. Evaluate the specific task you want it to perform rather than assuming a general agent includes a complete relationship-management workflow.

## Where ReacherX belongs in this choice

ReacherX is for a person who needs relevant people and wants help finding them, checking fit, approaching them, and following through. Customers are one use case. Candidates, investors, partners, research participants, creators, community members, and podcast guests are others.

Workspaces separate those jobs. Profiles bring together available context, plans, progress, and supported conversations. You can save instructions, prepare individual plans for several people, and inspect Analytics and △ Agent activity as work happens.

The combination is the reason to try it. AI, plain language, memory, and contact management each exist elsewhere too. ReacherX is open source and still beta, so check [current pricing](/pricing) and try one real goal before deciding it belongs in your daily work.
