Finding the right people starts with a clear idea of who you want to reach. It also takes practice: choosing useful criteria, reviewing evidence, and learning from the results.

This blog is a place to make that work easier.

## Practical guides

Our tutorials will walk through real workflows, from describing your audience to reviewing the people you find. Start with [writing a useful targeting brief](/blog/write-a-better-targeting-brief).

## Notes from engineering

ReacherX is open source. We’ll share implementation decisions, code, and lessons from building the product. You can explore the [source on GitHub](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x).

## Product updates

Announcements will explain what changed and how to use it. For a chronological record of releases, visit [GitHub releases](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/releases).

If a workflow needs a clearer explanation, [open an issue](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/issues). A useful question often makes a useful tutorial.
