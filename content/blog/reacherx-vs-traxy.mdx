A useful person may already be commenting on the right posts. The work is spotting them, checking why they matter, and deciding how to start a conversation.

traxy and ReacherX overlap in finding people from social activity. They package the work differently.

## Where traxy helps you look

[traxy](https://traxy.ai/) describes monitoring LinkedIn profiles, finding people who engage with their posts, matching them to your ideal customer, enriching contact details, and routing leads to Slack or a webhook. Its page also describes an agent that scans for matching buyers automatically.

That is a relevant choice if LinkedIn engagement is the source you want to monitor. Try a profile whose audience you understand well. Look at who appears and which activity caused them to appear.

Engaging with a post does not prove someone is ready to buy. Whatever product finds the person, read the context before making that leap.

## What ReacherX does with a broader request

You can start ReacherX with a goal such as finding event organizers who have recently asked for speakers. Describe the evidence you want and any exclusions.

△ Agent looks across X/Twitter and LinkedIn, weighs each person against your requirements, and collects the posts and activity behind the match. Qualification means reading what someone has actually said and done, not just their name and job title. You should still read the reason for a match and correct the audience when the results aren't useful.

The same workflow can serve hiring, partners, investors, creators, or community invitations. Workspaces keep the requirements and progress of those jobs separate. Each use case changes △ Agent's context as well as the names shown in the app.

## Decide where the work should go next

If your process already receives leads in Slack and handles outreach elsewhere, test how traxy's routing fits that process. Check that a routed lead includes enough context for the person taking over.

In ReacherX, you can keep working from the profile. Ask for an individual outreach plan, edit it with △ Agent, inspect the approval, and continue a supported DM conversation. A relevant public reply may be a better first step than a cold message.

Save an instruction when it should apply again. For instance: “For speaker invitations, explain the format and time commitment before asking them to commit.” That instruction belongs to the workspace where the work happens.

## Check a few people all the way through

Use a small set of results to judge both tools. Finding ten plausible names is only useful if you can understand the evidence and prepare an appropriate next step. Review one poor match as carefully as a good one; it shows you what needs correcting.
