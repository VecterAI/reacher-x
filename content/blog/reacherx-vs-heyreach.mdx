If you already have a list of LinkedIn contacts and need to run outreach across several sender accounts, HeyReach is worth evaluating. If you're starting with “I need to find the right people” and want help finding them, checking fit, and deciding how to approach them, look at ReacherX's workflow.

That's a more useful distinction than counting AI features or checking whether each product can send a voice note.

## What HeyReach is built to manage

[HeyReach](https://www.heyreach.io/) describes a LinkedIn campaign workflow: import contacts, combine outreach steps, rotate senders, and manage replies in a unified inbox. It supports messages, connection requests, voice notes, and other LinkedIn actions.

For an agency managing LinkedIn campaigns across accounts, that focus matters. You need to know which account sends what and where the replies will be handled.

HeyReach also documents AI and MCP connections. It isn't limited to manually written campaign steps.

## What ReacherX brings together

ReacherX begins with your purpose and the people who would fit it. You might be finding interview participants today and an engineer to hire tomorrow. Those can live in separate workspaces with different requirements, instructions, and progress stages.

△ Agent can discover people on X/Twitter and LinkedIn, check their fit, gather details, and prepare an individual outreach plan. You can review the evidence on a profile, edit a message, attach relevant media, and continue supported DMs in the app.

For example, a podcast host could look for engineers who have written about a difficult migration. The useful result is not merely a LinkedIn contact. It is someone whose experience fits the episode and an invitation that explains why.

The hosted app doesn't require coding or building an automation from scratch. You explain the task and review the work. [Saved instructions](/blog/teach-reacherx-what-you-want) let you adapt how △ Agent approaches it over time.

## Be precise about inboxes and voice notes

| Question                                 | ReacherX v4 beta                                       | HeyReach                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Can I work with X/Twitter and LinkedIn?          | Both are supported in the application                  | Its documented campaign focus is LinkedIn                        |
| Where do I read DMs?                     | In an individual person's supported conversation       | Unified LinkedIn inbox across sender accounts                    |
| Are voice notes available?               | Record your own voice in supported conversations       | Static recordings and AI voice notes are documented              |
| Is this built for many LinkedIn senders? | Do not assume a workspace is a separate sender account | Sender rotation and multi-account campaigns are central features |

HeyReach's [voice-note guide](https://help.heyreach.io/en/articles/16790621-ai-voice-notes-overview) describes static recordings and personalized AI-generated audio. ReacherX's [voice-note guide](/blog/send-voice-notes) covers your own microphone recording. Neither “has voice notes” nor “has an inbox” establishes a unique feature.

ReacherX works on individual conversations, not a shared team inbox; a unified inbox is still planned.

## Pick the workflow you actually need

If sender management and LinkedIn campaign operations are your main problem, test HeyReach with those requirements. If you need to go from a goal to relevant people and individual conversations, try ReacherX with one real goal.

During the trial, check the account setup, quality of matches, review controls, and where you handle replies. Also check how easily you can keep two unrelated workspaces apart. Don't judge either product by a demo that skips those steps.
