Building solo sucks and is lonely. I always wanted friends with whom I could build cool things. In my real life, I couldn't find anyone who was excited about the things I was excited about, or someone who could stand shoulder to shoulder.

When you're building alone, there's no one to keep you in check or guide you. You have a higher chance of ending up in the wrong place and going down a rabbit hole. So by open-sourcing ReacherX, my goal is to connect with people who care about the things I care about. I want to talk, exchange ideas, and learn from other people. I want to ask questions and answer questions.

I've been working on this since mid-2023. I was studying for my BSCS at the time, working part time, and freelancing on Upwork as a designer and developer. Each time I tried to go full time, I ran low on cash and had to do something to make money. This June, I left my last company and finally went full time on ReacherX.

There's a lot left to do. The idea of telling a tool what people you need and having it connect you with the right people sounds simple. Building it has taken me more than three years, and v4 is still beta. I want people to help improve these foundations and adapt them to their own needs.

## Come talk to me

[Join the Discord server](https://discord.gg/BQttyr8jY). Tell me what interests you, or what you tried and couldn't get working. You don't need to arrive with a pull request.

I would like help from people who understand parts of this better than I do. Search, AI agents, background jobs, interface design, testing, documentation: there's work in all of them. There are also decisions I'd like to talk through before writing more code.

## Where to look in the code

Start with the [architecture overview](/blog/how-reacherx-agent-works). It explains the three layers: tools △ Agent can call, workflows that coordinate jobs, and core functions that do the work.

Then pick the part you care about:

- [Discovery](/blog/how-reacherx-discovery-works): where possible matches come from.
- [Qualification](/blog/how-reacherx-qualification-works): how the system decides whether someone fits.
- [Enrichment](/blog/how-reacherx-enrichment-works): turning outside data into a useful profile.
- [Planning](/blog/how-reacherx-planning-works) and [outreach](/blog/how-reacherx-outreach-runs): preparing and carrying out the next steps.
- [Memory](/blog/how-reacherx-memory-works): saved instructions and feedback.
- [Reporting](/blog/how-reacherx-reporting-works): where the numbers come from.

Each article points to files you can open. The [setup guide](/blog/run-reacherx-yourself) covers running a copy locally. If you get stuck, tell me which step failed and what it printed. That helps me fix the instructions too.

## Before spending a week on a change

Check the [issues](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/issues) and read [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). Please discuss new features, roadmap work, integrations, agent-behavior changes, architecture changes, and major UX changes with me first. A small bug fix or typo doesn't need a long conversation.

For a bug, show how to reproduce it. Explain what changed and how you checked the fix. Screenshots help with interface changes. Search for an existing helper before adding another one.

If coding isn't your thing, try the app with a goal you understand and tell me which results were wrong. Clear feedback, a better instruction, or a walkthrough can help someone else use it.

And if you want to help me keep working on this full time, there's [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/cw/ReacherX). You can also star [the repository](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x) or share it with a friend who might want to build with us.
