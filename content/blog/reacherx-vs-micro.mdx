ReacherX is an open-source tool for finding people you haven't met yet and managing the outreach that follows. You describe who fits your goal, and it searches, checks, and prepares one approach per person.

Email and calendar are on my roadmap for ReacherX. Micro already brings those into its app. If you're looking at these two products because you need help with your inbox, that difference matters immediately.

Both have an AI assistant and a way to keep track of people. Neither hosted product requires you to be a developer. Here is what I would look at when deciding between them. The terms below follow [how ReacherX works](/blog/how-reacherx-works), the short guide to the product's vocabulary.

## What you get today

| Job                                     | Micro                                                         | ReacherX v4 beta                                                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Work with email and calendar            | Included in its product                                       | Planned                                                          |
| Keep contact records                    | CRM, including contacts from synced email and calendar events | Profiles for the people you find, with stages and outreach plans |
| Give written instructions               | AI assistant                                                  | AI agent                                                         |
| Keep documents and tasks with your work | Both are listed in its documentation                          | Outreach plans organize the steps for approaching a person       |

The facts above come from [Micro documentation](https://docs.micro.so/) and [the FAQ](https://docs.micro.so/account/faq), checked September 10, 2026. The FAQ lists Gmail support and says Outlook is planned.

## Try this in Micro

Pick a person you already work with. Can you find the email, the meeting, and the notes you need before speaking again? Check how contacts are created from your connected accounts and how much you need to organize yourself.

That would tell you something useful about whether Micro fits your day. I haven't measured the time it saves or tested its assistant against ReacherX's.

## Try this in ReacherX

Pick someone you need to find. For example, you might need speakers who have worked on web accessibility. Tell △ Agent, the AI that does the searching and drafting, what experience matters. It searches X/Twitter and LinkedIn, checks possible matches, and gathers background.

Read a profile, then ask for a plan to approach the person. A plan is the drafted approach for reaching that one person, which you can review and change. You can change the plan and continue available X/Twitter or LinkedIn conversations from that profile. Select several people if you want an individual plan for each.

Keep the event in its own workspace. A workspace is the container for one goal, and a hiring search can have another one with different people, requirements, plans, and saved instructions. Social accounts you connect stay shared across your account, so you link X/Twitter or LinkedIn once rather than per workspace. The [CRM guide](/blog/manage-people-with-reacherx) explains the stages.

So which one is for you? Micro, if your day mostly runs through the email, calendar, and documents you already have. ReacherX, if it starts with people you haven't found yet.
