ReacherX and Xreacher are separate products. If you've seen both names while looking for help with outreach, the useful distinction is how you want to work with people.

Do you have a campaign to run across social accounts, or a particular kind of person to find and a relationship to work on?

## What Xreacher offers

[Xreacher's site](https://xreacher.com/) describes outreach across X, LinkedIn, and Telegram. It lists lead scraping, AI message suggestions, automated follow-ups, campaign analytics, and an AI chatbot for incoming replies.

Xreacher also supports Telegram. ReacherX currently supports outreach on X and LinkedIn.

If managing campaigns across those platforms is your main need, evaluate that workflow directly. Inspect how targeting, sender selection, follow-ups, and reply handling fit together in the plan you would buy.

## How ReacherX approaches a person

In ReacherX, you begin with a workspace goal. For a podcast, that might be finding guests who have built developer communities from scratch. The agent searches supported sources, checks fit, and gathers the background you can read.

You can ask for a plan for one person or select several people and give the agent instructions for each. Each plan belongs to a person. A useful first step might be a relevant public reply, followed by a wait, rather than an immediate DM.

The profile keeps the available evidence, plan, progress, and supported conversation together. Your podcast work can stay separate from hiring or finding partners, with different criteria and saved preferences.

## Look at the reply, not only the send

A reply can change what makes sense next. ReacherX can revisit a person’s plan when new replies or interactions arrive. It can continue, finish, or stop that plan. The agent can still misunderstand what someone meant, so read the conversation and check the proposed next step.

Start in review-required mode and inspect the proposed work. If you later enable autonomous sending, understand that supported messages can go out without another approval. You still need to correct bad assumptions and keep real-world outcomes accurate.

You can use images or video where supported, and record your own voice notes in eligible chats. The [media guide](/blog/outreach-with-images-and-video) explains when those help someone understand you.

## Use the same goal to judge the result

Try a small audience before scaling anything. Read why each person fits, inspect the proposed message, and see how easy it is to stop inappropriate work. A high send count by itself doesn't show that an introduction was welcome.
