A CRM is a tool for keeping track of people and your conversations with them. The name stands for customer relationship management. In ReacherX, those people don't have to be customers. A person is anyone the system saved into a workspace as a possible match, and the workspace is the container for one purpose, holding its people, plans, instructions, and progress together.

If those words are new, [How ReacherX works](/blog/how-reacherx-works) gives the short version of each before you go further.

I am a developer and designer, not a traditional salesperson. I don't want someone to have to learn sales terminology just to use the app. If you're hiring, you should be looking at candidates. If you're trying to raise money, you should be looking at investors. That's the reason the management part of ReacherX changes with the job you're doing.

## Where do I keep everything?

Start with one workspace per purpose. For example, if you describe hiring an engineer, ReacherX sets the workspace up for recruiting and uses that when finding people, deciding whether they fit, and preparing outreach. You add the requirements for your particular role.

Open a person's profile to see why they matched, their background, their plan, and their supported X/Twitter or LinkedIn conversation. A plan is the prepared approach for that person, including the message. You can read the exchange there before asking △ Agent, the assistant that carries out the work, what to do next.

If you're also looking for investors, [make another workspace](/blog/workspaces-explained). Its people, plans, memories, and reports stay separate. Your connected social accounts are still shared across your user account. A new workspace isn't a new account to send from.

## How do I know where things stand?

Each workspace comes with stages that match its purpose. A stage is the label for where a person stands in your work, such as Sourced, Interviewing, and Hired for hiring. Change the stage from the person's profile or card menu when something happens. If you hire someone after a call, mark them Hired. ReacherX won't know the outcome of a call you had outside the app.

Partnerships have Negotiating and Partnered stages. Investor outreach has In discussion and Committed. These labels come with the use case. You can't create a completely custom set of stages yet.

## What can I ask △ Agent to do?

For a candidate you want to approach, try:

> Prepare an introduction for this person. Mention the salary range. I want to find out whether they're open to a conversation about the role.

Review the plan and change what doesn't fit. You can [tag several people in one request](/blog/create-plans-for-several-people) to prepare separate plans for a shortlist.

You can also [ask △ Agent to remember an instruction](/blog/teach-reacherx-what-you-want), such as always including the salary range. Memory is the instruction the agent saves and reuses in this workspace. Replies and other interactions can cause ReacherX to reconsider a plan and continue, finish, or stop it.

Sending requires your review by default. [Autonomous mode](/blog/what-reacherx-does-automatically) allows supported messages to go out without another approval. Archiving a person triggers a pause of their automated work.

When you come back to the app, check replies and pending approvals, then update anything that happened elsewhere. A completed plan and a successful hire are different things.
