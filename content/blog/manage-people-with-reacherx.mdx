A CRM is a tool for keeping track of people and your conversations with them. The name stands for customer relationship management. In ReacherX, those people don't have to be customers.

I am a developer and designer, not a traditional salesperson. I don't want someone to have to learn sales terminology just to use the app. If you're hiring, you should be looking at candidates. If you're trying to raise money, you should be looking at investors. That's the reason the management part of ReacherX changes with the job you're doing.

## Where do I keep everything?

Start with a workspace for one project. For example, choose recruiting if you want to hire an engineer. ReacherX uses that choice when finding people, deciding whether they fit, and preparing outreach. You add the requirements for your particular role.

Open a person's profile to see why they matched, their background, their plan, and their supported X/Twitter or LinkedIn conversation. You can read the exchange there before asking △ Agent what to do next.

If you're also looking for investors, [make another workspace](/blog/workspaces-explained). Its people, plans, memories, and reports stay separate. Your connected social accounts are still shared across your user account; a new workspace isn't a new account to send from.

## How do I know where things stand?

For a hiring project, you have stages such as Sourced, Interviewing, and Hired. Change the stage from the person's profile or card menu when something happens. If you hire someone after a call, mark them Hired. ReacherX won't know the outcome of a call you had outside the app.

Partnerships have Negotiating and Partnered stages. Investor outreach has In discussion and Committed. These labels come with the use case; you can't create a completely custom set of stages yet.

## What can I ask △ Agent to do?

For a candidate you want to approach, try:

> Prepare an introduction for this person. Mention the salary range. I want to find out whether they're open to a conversation about the role.

Review the plan and change what doesn't fit. You can [tag several people in one request](/blog/create-plans-for-several-people) to prepare separate plans for a shortlist.

You can also [ask △ Agent to remember an instruction](/blog/teach-reacherx-what-you-want), such as always including the salary range. Replies and other interactions can cause ReacherX to reconsider a plan and continue, finish, or stop it.

Sending requires your review by default. [Autonomous mode](/blog/what-reacherx-does-automatically) allows supported messages to go out without another approval. Archiving a person triggers a pause of their automated work.

When you come back to the app, check replies and pending approvals, then update anything that happened elsewhere. A completed plan and a successful hire are different things.

This is still v4 beta. Email sending, calendar integrations, and a unified inbox are planned. Supported X/Twitter and LinkedIn chats are available inside individual profiles today.
