You shouldn't have to learn how a data provider works just to find somebody worth talking to. Bond and ReacherX both try to remove that work from the person's way.

Once research is done, though, you need somewhere to take the next step.

## What Bond prepares

[Bond](https://www.askbond.ai/) calls itself a personal GTM engineer. You describe your ideal customer and the signals you care about (its site lists job changes, funding, and hiring intent), and Bond builds the list, verifies every contact, and researches each prospect. It says it draws on 50+ data providers with results cross-verified, then prepares emails, LinkedIn messages, and call scripts you can push to a sequencer in one click. A sequencer is a tool for sending a planned series of messages.

Bond's site says you can get started without an engineer, and that you approve everything before it sends. Both tools let you describe what you want in plain language.

If you want researched contacts and message preparation feeding an existing sales process, test how the prepared work moves into it. Check the actual fields and message content you receive, and confirm what sends the message after export.

## What stays together in ReacherX

In ReacherX, the person stays at the center after research: their profile connects the evidence, the outreach plan, the progress, and the supported conversation. Separate workspaces carry separate use cases: a partner search uses Negotiating and Partnered rather than Interviewing and Hired. The [CRM guide](/blog/manage-people-with-reacherx) shows how that setup works.

## Try changing the plan

Pick a result you would actually contact. Ask for a short first message that explains the mutual benefit. Then add a constraint: “Don't ask for a call yet. First check whether their audience would want this.”

Can you read the revised plan and understand what changed? Saved workspace instructions and separate plans for selected people let you keep that preference without writing a workflow.

△ Agent can also run supported outreach steps. Review is required by default; autonomous sending is an explicit setting. [Learn the controls](/blog/what-reacherx-does-automatically) before handing over sending.

## Choose around the work you already have

If your main need is preparing data and copy for a sales sequencer, inspect Bond's current workflow. If you want to find people, manage individual plans, and continue supported social conversations in the same app, try ReacherX with one concrete goal.
