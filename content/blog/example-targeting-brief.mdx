## Start with a situation

“Small businesses” describes a market. It does not tell you which conversation to look for. A useful brief names a person, a problem, and the moment when that problem matters.

**Broad:** founders who might need a CRM.  
**Specific:** founders who are losing track of follow-ups after their first sales calls.

### Write the first version

1. Name the person who does the work.
   - Include their role, such as a founder or sales lead.
   - Leave out details that do not change the conversation.
2. Describe a problem they have mentioned.
   - Look for a concrete task: remembering a follow-up, sharing notes, or reviewing a reply.
   - Avoid guessing their budget or purchase timeline.
3. Explain what would make a result irrelevant.

#### Include an exclusion

A job posting for a sales lead is not a request for sales software. Write down that distinction before collecting results.

#### Keep the language familiar

Use the words a customer would type. “I forgot to follow up” is more useful than “relationship lifecycle optimization.”

## Compare the two briefs

| Part     | Too broad          | More useful                                            |
| -------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Person   | Any business owner | A founder handling the first sales calls               |
| Problem  | Needs growth       | Follow-ups get lost between a spreadsheet and an inbox |
| Evidence | Works at a startup | Describes a missed follow-up in a public post          |

<BlogCallout title="A brief is a starting point">

Read the full conversation before deciding that someone is a fit. A matching phrase is a reason to look, not a reason to send a pitch.

</BlogCallout>

## Review before using it

- [x] The role is clear.
- [x] The problem can be observed in a conversation.
- [ ] The exclusions have been checked against a few real examples.
- [ ] Someone else can explain the brief in one sentence.

Use **specific evidence**, keep assumptions _tentative_, and remove ~~every possible audience~~ anything that does not belong in this search.
