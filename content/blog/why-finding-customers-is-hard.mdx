You can build a useful product and still struggle to find someone who needs it. I kept running into that gap. It is the problem I wanted ReacherX to solve.

This article adapts a thread I wrote in March 2025. It is my view of customer acquisition and the reasons behind the product.

## The work starts before you meet a customer

Outreach, search traffic, content, and advertising all offer ways to reach people. Each asks something different of you.

Writing content takes time. Search visibility takes patience. Ads require a budget, creative work, and repeated testing. Outreach asks you to find the right person and understand why they might care.

For a founder or freelancer doing this alone, that can become a second job.

## Paying to learn is expensive

My frustration with ads starts with the order of events. You spend money before you know whether a campaign will bring you customers.

Then you write the ads, watch performance, adjust targeting, and try again. Platforms give you reports, but those reports do not give you a conversation with each person who saw an ad.

I wanted to start with something more concrete. Show me the person, the problem they described, and the context. Let me decide whether I can help.

## Start with a need someone has already expressed

A public post asking for a recommendation is a useful starting point. So is a complaint about a workflow your product improves.

That is why I was more interested in searching for customer needs than buying another round of impressions. A real conversation gives you something specific to respond to.

> I want to read what someone needs before I decide what to say to them.

This does not make ads useless or outreach effortless. It changes where the work begins.

## Why I started building ReacherX

The original idea was a search engine for finding customers. Describe what you offer, find people talking about a matching need, and understand the conversation before reaching out.

The goal was to make that work accessible to people who do not have an acquisition team or the time to become advertising specialists.

That March thread introduced the idea. [The product vision](/blog/finding-customers-should-be-easier) explains what I wanted to build around it.
