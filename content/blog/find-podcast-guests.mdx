A guest who has just done the thing you want to discuss often beats the most famous name in the field. The story is still fresh for them, and for your listeners. ReacherX helps you find that person. It searches X/Twitter and LinkedIn for people who match a description you write, and this guide uses that to fill an episode.

Start from the episode idea, then look for the person who can make the conversation worth listening to. If the vocabulary is new, [How ReacherX works](/blog/how-reacherx-works) defines workspace, plan, agent, and memory in plain words. For this guide, remember that the people ReacherX finds land in your workspace, each with the posts and background that explain the match.

## Search around the episode

For an episode about going from freelance work to a small agency, you could ask ReacherX.

> Find people on X/Twitter and LinkedIn who have shared their experience hiring their first teammate after freelancing. I'm looking for a podcast guest who can talk about what changed and what went wrong.

Read what the results have shared. Is there enough to build a conversation around? Can you think of two or three questions you'd want to ask this person?

If you can't, narrow the episode idea before reaching out. A narrower idea also makes the invitation easier to write. △ Agent, the AI agent that runs your workspace, follows your description when it searches, so a sharper description returns sharper results.

## Put the useful details in the invitation

Explain the topic, format, and time involved. For example:

> I host a podcast for freelancers. Your post about hiring your first designer raised a question I'd like to explore: how do you hand over client work without reviewing every detail? Would you be open to a 30-minute recorded conversation about it?

Add a link to an episode so they can hear what the show is like. Mention whether the recording includes video and where you'll publish it.

You can ask ReacherX to [help prepare the invitation](/blog/create-plans-for-several-people), but keep the question that made you interested in this guest. A plan is a set of draft messages for one person, and it works best when the reason you picked them survives the drafting.

## Make saying yes easy

Offer a few possible times and explain what preparation, if any, you need. If they ask about your audience, give an honest answer.

Use [the conversation](/blog/manage-dm-conversations) to follow up and answer questions. If a guest mentions they can only record in the evenings, ask △ Agent to remember that for this workspace, so it doesn't suggest a morning slot next week.
