I want to share the roadmap I have in mind for ReacherX so far. It can change based on feedback and what I learn as the project progresses, but I want people to know what I'm thinking about. Maybe some of these are things you'd like to help build.

I started this while studying for my computer science degree, working part time, and freelancing. Each time I ran low on money, I had to do paid work and continue with ReacherX on the side. In June 2026, I left my last company and went full time. I want to keep it that way.

## The things I want to add

A unified inbox is one of them. Right now, you open a person's profile to get to their conversation. I want you to be able to get to your conversations across platforms from one place, without opening people one by one.

I also want to add email sending using your own Gmail or Outlook account. You should be able to write and send it yourself, or ask the agent to do it. Google Calendar and Outlook Calendar integrations are on my list too, for when a conversation turns into a meeting.

Then there's an MCP server. That would let you use ReacherX in Codex, Claude, Cursor, or another tool that supports MCP. I'm a developer and designer myself, and I take a lot of inspiration from Cursor. Being able to work with ReacherX from the tools you already use makes sense to me.

And I want a mobile app. You shouldn't have to sit at a computer just to pick up a conversation.

These are plans. They aren't available in v4 beta today, and I haven't promised dates for them.

## There is still work to do on what's here

Finding the right people accurately has been the biggest challenge with ReacherX. Adding more ways to contact someone won't help much if the person shouldn't have been suggested in the first place.

The [repository roadmap](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) includes work on testing search results and plans. Setup, error messages, and the experience of running the app yourself also need work. I don't want to leave those things behind while adding features.

## Tell me what you need

I am the only person building this right now. I can't do everything at once, and I don't think I can figure out every use case on my own either.

If you've tried ReacherX, tell me what you were trying to do and what stopped you. That could change what I work on next.

If you want to build a feature, integration, roadmap item, or change to the agent, [get in touch first](https://github.com/VecterAI/reacher-x/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). The same applies to architecture and major UX changes. Small fixes, typos, and focused polish don't need a long discussion.

I've written about [supporting development](/blog/support-reacherx) if you want to help me stay full time, and [how the agent works](/blog/how-reacherx-agent-works) if you want to get into the code.
