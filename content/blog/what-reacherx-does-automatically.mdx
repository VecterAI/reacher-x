When I released v3, I realised I had spent too long building for an idea of software that was already changing. People expected the tool to do the work for them. I had built something they still had to spend too much time working with.

That was a big reason for making v4 agentic. By that, I mean you explain what you're trying to do and △ Agent can carry out work toward it. You shouldn't have to sit there moving every step along.

ReacherX is still beta, so I want to be clear about what that means in the app today.

## What can it do while I'm away?

Once a workspace is set up and running, it can keep finding people, checking whether they fit, gathering background, and preparing outreach plans. Closing the browser doesn't stop those jobs.

Supported replies and other activity can cause △ Agent to reconsider a plan. It might continue, finish, or stop it. A plan can also wait until a scheduled time or ask you for input.

You can see what's happening in the workspace status. Sometimes one part can keep working while another needs attention. For example, discovery might be running while a messaging account needs reconnecting. Usage limits and pauses can stop work too.

## Will it send messages without asking me?

By default, sending approvals are on. You review plans and messages through the approval flow. I'd leave that on at first. Read what it wants to send and make sure it understands the people you're looking for.

You can switch sending approvals off in the workspace's agent settings. Then supported replies and DMs can send without another approval. The confirmation shows whether existing draft plans will start as well. Read that count, because the change can affect work you've already prepared.

## What do I still need to do?

Tell it when something is wrong. Give it a useful correction and [ask it to remember the instruction](/blog/teach-reacherx-what-you-want). If it keeps finding the wrong people, change the audience requirements. If you've changed your offer, tell it what the new offer is.

Keep track of things that happen elsewhere, too. A person accepting an invitation on a phone call won't automatically become a recorded outcome in ReacherX. Finishing an outreach plan doesn't prove they accepted.

And you can stop the work. Open the workspace status and choose “Pause △ Agent”. This pauses new discovery, monitoring, and queued background work for that workspace. A task already running may finish safely. Your saved people and progress stay there, and you can resume from the same dialog.

I want ReacherX to do more of the work over time. I also want you to be able to see what it's doing. [Analytics](/blog/read-your-reacherx-analytics) shows recorded outcomes. [Agent observability](/blog/understand-agent-observability) lets you inspect the activity behind them.
