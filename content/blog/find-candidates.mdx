ReacherX finds people on X/Twitter and LinkedIn who fit what you describe, then helps you start a conversation with them. This guide is the hiring version of that job. You want your first teammate, so you describe the work you need help with, look at the people ReacherX finds, and send an invitation that makes sense to them.

Two of the product's words come up in every guide. A workspace is where ReacherX keeps one job, with its own people and messages. A plan is the set of draft messages and steps for approaching one person. If you want the full tour, [How ReacherX works](/blog/how-reacherx-works) explains workspace, person, plan, and memory in plain words.

## Search for the work, not just the title

"Frontend engineer" covers a lot of different work. Someone who builds landing pages may not want a job untangling a large application's keyboard navigation.

Before looking for your first hire, write down what you actually need help with. Then ask △ Agent, the AI agent that runs your workspace. Give it the role in your own words.

> Help me find frontend engineers on X/Twitter and LinkedIn who have shared work on accessibility or complex web apps. We're a small team hiring someone to improve our product's UI. They need to work within three hours of our time zone.

Change those details to match the real role. If a requirement is optional, say so. You don't want a useful person excluded because you listed every nice-to-have as essential.

When △ Agent finds someone who might fit, it saves that person in your workspace, together with the posts and background that explain the match. Open the results and look at what they've made or written. Was it their project? What did they contribute? Would you want to ask them about it in an interview?

## Give them the role details early

A message like "Exciting opportunity, interested?" asks someone to reply before telling them anything useful.

Try an opening that explains why you're contacting them:

> I read your post about making that settings screen work with a keyboard. We're hiring someone to do similar work on our app. It's a remote role on a team of three. Can I send you the details?

Share pay, hours, and responsibilities when you can. Those details help someone decide whether the conversation is worth their time.

If you catch yourself repeating the same instruction, such as "remote candidates only", ask △ Agent to remember it for this workspace. A saved instruction like that is called a memory, and later searches and drafts in the same workspace take it into account.

## Keep track of the people you want to speak with

Because your description is about hiring, ReacherX sets the workspace up for recruiting. The people you find are called candidates there, and each one moves through hiring stages such as Sourced, Interviewing, and Hired. Your shortlist stays organized while you decide who's worth a real conversation.

You can [prepare separate plans for several candidates](/blog/create-plans-for-several-people) and [continue their DMs in the app](/blog/manage-dm-conversations).
