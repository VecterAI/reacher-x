Someone visits your pricing page, tries your product, and later comments on a relevant post. For a sales team, connecting those events may be more useful than discovering another hundred names.

Someone organizing their first research study has a different starting point. They may have no visitors, customer data, or sales team at all.

## Where Common Room fits

[Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io/) combines buyer intelligence, enrichment, signals, AI agents, and workflows. Its product page describes connecting fragmented activity and identities into people, accounts, and buying context.

That is a meaningful reason to evaluate it if your problem is understanding buyers across the information your organization already has. Ask for a demonstration using the sources you actually use. See whether you can trace an apparent buying signal back to the underlying event.

Common Room has AI agents. Calling ReacherX agentic doesn't establish an advantage by itself.

## Where ReacherX starts earlier

In ReacherX, you can begin with a purpose and an audience description. For a research study, explain the experience participants need and what would rule someone out. △ Agent looks for possible matches on X/Twitter and LinkedIn, checks them against the requirements, and gathers available details.

Open a result and read the evidence. A post about the subject may indicate relevant experience, but it doesn't mean the person wants to join your study. You still have to make a sensible invitation.

The same application supports hiring, partnerships, creators, investors, and other people-focused goals. Workspaces keep their requirements, plans, memories, and progress separate.

## Keep the evidence close to the action

A score is easier to trust when you can explain it. In ReacherX, review the person's profile before generating or approving outreach. If the person doesn't fit, correct the audience or reject the result instead of polishing a message for them.

You can ask for an individual plan, revise it with △ Agent, and continue supported conversations in the app. Analytics helps you inspect outcomes; Agent observability exposes the work behind discovery and memory. Neither replaces reading a few actual results.

This is useful when you want one place to work from an unfamiliar name to a relevant conversation. It doesn't make ReacherX a substitute for every data source or revenue workflow a larger organization uses.

## Decide which gap you need to close

If you need to combine buying activity across an existing business, make that your Common Room evaluation. If you need to find and reach suitable people for a specific project, try that project in ReacherX.

ReacherX remains v4 beta. It focuses on supported X/Twitter and LinkedIn outreach, with email sending and a unified inbox still planned.
