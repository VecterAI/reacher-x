I don't want ReacherX to stop being useful once you've found someone. The whole reason for finding the person was to talk to them. So X/Twitter and LinkedIn conversations are built into their profiles.

You can read their background, open the conversation, and reply there. You don't have to copy their name and go looking for them again in another app.

## Open the conversation from the profile

Choose the person in your workspace, then open their conversation. You'll see the messages your connected account can access. Read what's there before writing.

Maybe you prepared an introduction yesterday, but they've already replied with a question today. Answer the question. The old introduction doesn't need to be sent just because you'd planned it.

## What can I send?

You can type a reply yourself. [Autocomplete](/blog/write-with-autocomplete) can help with the next few words, or you can work with △ Agent on a draft.

You can attach supported media and [record your own voice note](/blog/send-voice-notes) too. The controls depend on the platform and connection. Some X features need an unlocked X Chat session.

After sending, check that the message appears without an error. If the connection drops, look at the conversation before sending it again. The first attempt might have gone through.

## Where is the inbox?

Right now, you open each conversation from the person's profile. A unified inbox across people and platforms is still on [the roadmap](/blog/where-reacherx-is-going).

There can also be limits on the history your connection can fetch. If an old message seems to be missing, check X/Twitter or LinkedIn itself. A gap here doesn't mean the conversation never happened.

This is one of the reasons ReacherX is a mix of different things. Finding people, understanding who they are, and talking to them belong together. I want a person who has never used a sales tool to be able to do all of that without learning a whole new way of working.
