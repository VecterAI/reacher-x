Finding someone is only half the job. At some point you talk to them, and ReacherX keeps that part in the app too. A person in ReacherX is someone the system saved into your workspace as a possible match, with their profile, evidence, and plan in one place. Their conversation is the X/Twitter or LinkedIn message thread, opened through your connected account, which is your own social account linked to ReacherX.

If you're new to the app, [How ReacherX works](/blog/how-reacherx-works) explains those terms and the path a person travels from first result to conversation.

You can read their background, open the conversation, and reply there. You don't have to copy their name and go looking for them again in another app.

## Open the conversation from the profile

Choose the person in your workspace, then open their conversation. You'll see the messages your connected account can access. Read what's there before writing.

Maybe you prepared an introduction yesterday, but they've already replied with a question today. Answer the question. The old plan's message doesn't need to be sent just because you'd planned it.

## What can I send?

You can type a reply yourself, let autocomplete help with the next few words, or work with △ Agent on a draft.

You can attach supported media and [record your own voice note](/blog/send-voice-notes) too. Which controls appear depends on the platform and connection, and some X features need an unlocked X Chat session. X Chat messages are encrypted, so you confirm the PIN that protects them before those controls work.

After sending, check that the message appears without an error. If the connection drops, look at the conversation before sending it again. The first attempt might have gone through.

## Where is the inbox?

Right now, you open each conversation from the person's profile. A unified inbox across people and platforms is still on [the roadmap](/blog/where-reacherx-is-going).

There can also be limits on the history your connection can fetch. If an old message seems to be missing, check X/Twitter or LinkedIn itself. A gap here doesn't mean the conversation never happened.

This is one of the reasons ReacherX is a mix of different things. Finding people, understanding who they are, and talking to them belong together.
