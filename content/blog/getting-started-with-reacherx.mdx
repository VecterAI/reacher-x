I want a normal person to be able to use ReacherX without having to learn sales or have expertise in marketing. I'm a developer and designer myself, and I'm trying to keep things simple enough that anyone can get value from the app.

You do need to explain what you want, though. ReacherX won't know what makes someone useful to you until you tell it. For this walkthrough, let's use hiring: you need an engineer for a contract project involving payments in a web app.

## Go through setup with one job in mind

The setup steps are “Who to reach?”, “Your audience”, “Connect accounts”, “Plans”, and “Preferences”. Choose recruiting for this example. Explain the work and the experience the person needs.

You could start with:

> I need an engineer for a contract project. They should have experience building checkout or payment features for web apps. Help me find people whose work shows that experience. I want to ask whether they're available for a conversation about the project.

Be clear about requirements and preferences. If a particular technology is required, say so. Add your website if it helps explain the project. Read the proposed audience before continuing and correct anything the agent misunderstood.

Follow the account-connection steps for the platforms you intend to use, then review the plan and preferences before finishing. The project you're setting up is called a workspace. [This guide explains workspaces](/blog/workspaces-explained) and how to keep different projects separate.

## Read the first results

Open someone's profile and look at why they matched. For this example, does the evidence show work on payments, or just a general engineering job title? It won't necessarily tell you whether they're available for a contract. That's something you may need to ask.

Tell the agent what missed the point. Ask it to update the audience and review the change. If the workspace needs attention, open its status and read the reason; an unfinished setup or disconnected account needs fixing before asking for more results.

For a person you'd like to contact, ask for a plan. Read the proposed message and change what doesn't fit. Keep approvals on while learning the app. That's the default; [autonomous mode](/blog/what-reacherx-does-automatically) can send supported messages without another approval.

## Save what you want it to remember

If you have an instruction for every introduction, ask the agent to remember it for this workspace. For example, you might always want to mention the contract length. Check that it confirms the instruction was saved.

On your next visit, read replies and work waiting for approval. Update anything that happened outside the app. [Analytics](/blog/read-your-reacherx-analytics) is there when you want to look at the wider picture.

ReacherX is still beta. If a step is confusing, [tell me in Discord](https://discord.gg/BQttyr8jY) what you were trying to do and where you got stuck. You don't need to know why it broke to give useful feedback.
