### ReacherX on company prompts

Of ReacherX's 325 matches for p14, none is a company:

- 235 are people: founders, CEOs and similar, at 115 distinct companies.
- 90 were not typed yet.
- 0 are organizations.

Discovery found personal LinkedIn and X profiles, not company accounts. The proposal says not to substitute a person for a company, so ReacherX scores "no results" on this prompt. This affects the 8 company prompts (13–20) of 20.

**Decided (18 Nov 2026): option B.** ReacherX finds the people behind companies, so on company prompts up to five distinct companies its qualified people work at, taken in the people's best-fit order, are also judged as companies, with the same judges and criteria as every tool. If a company fits, it counts as a match for ReacherX. It is reported as its own labelled row, `reacherx_via_people`, next to ReacherX's own results.
