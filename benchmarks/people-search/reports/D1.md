- **AI cost with the 20-per-cycle cap.** At this prompt's hit rate, about 27–40 checks reach 20 matches: about **$0.25–0.32 per cycle**, under the $0.65 limit. 100 matches per query means about 5 cycles, or **$1.25–1.60 per query**.
- **LinkedIn credits with the cap.** Each prospect checked costs 2 credits (profile and posts). Each match costs 3 more (profile and posts read again, plus contact info). On top of that come the finance search (up to 50 per cycle) and discovery (about 18).

  ```
  Projected Renidly credits per capped cycle
    Reaches 20 matches quickly  ████░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░  ~200
    Few matches                 ███████████░░░░░░░░░  ~500
    No matches                  ████████████████████  up to ~900
  ```
- **The risk.** The cap stops a cycle only once it has 20 matches. A cycle with few or no matches still checks everything discovery found. The pilot has to measure how often that happens on people prompts. The per-step credit and spend approvals stay as hard stops.

## 3. Runtime

- 129.3 active minutes for the uncapped cycle: 47.1 until the LinkedIn credit guard stopped it, then 82.8 after the resume.
- Discovery took about 6 minutes. Most of the time went to qualification, enrichment and lessons; LinkedIn calls are paced at about 17 seconds each.
- Capped cycles do far less work and should be much shorter. The pilot measures this.
